# Emails to send

Rewritten 2026-08-01, after the course-group material arrived. Three of the
earlier drafts asked questions that are now answered, and two asked the wrong
question entirely — the Information Retrieval one assumed there was no written
exam, and the Quantum one assumed the paper was multiple choice. Both were
wrong. These are the current versions.

**Before you send anything, two rules.**

1. **Addresses are not verified.** UniPV is usually `nome.cognome@unipv.it`,
   Statale `nome.cognome@unimi.it`, Bicocca `nome.cognome@unimib.it`, but
   exceptions are common. Look each one up on the departmental staff page.
2. **Fill in `[MATRICOLA]` and `[NOME COGNOME]`.** I have your codice fiscale,
   not your matricola, and I am not going to guess your name into an email to a
   professor.

Each mail asks one question and can be answered in one line. In August, that is
the difference between a reply and silence.

your final mark and a year's fees, and it is not published anywhere I can read.

> **Oggetto: Verifica posizione "in corso" per la sessione di laurea primaverile 2027**
>
> Gentile Segreteria,

> [NOME COGNOME]

*English:* I enrolled in 2023/24 on the three-year degree. If I graduate in the
spring 2027 session, do I still count as *in corso*, or does that session
require enrolling as *fuori corso* for 2026/27 with the associated fees?

## Do not send

**Bricolo — Cognitive Psychology (509485).** The course page asks previous-year
students to contact her *at least three days before the appello if they want the
current syllabus*. Staying silent keeps you on edition 7392: written threshold
12 rather than 13, and the final mark is the sum. Asking costs you a mark and
gains nothing. Say nothing.

## While you are at it

Nothing is bookable before **11 August**. On that day, book everything whose
window is open, then book each remaining exam as its window opens. The first
thing you can actually lose is Information Retrieval, and you lose it on
**26 August**.
