## Two things to verify before you rely on the plan

1. **Are there extraordinary October/December appelli for any of the deferred
   courses?** Project Work has 14/10 and 02/12, which proves the university
   does schedule them. If any of the eight January courses has one, January
   stops being a single attempt. The watcher looks for this.
2. **Do you still count as *in corso* if you graduate in spring 2027?** Your
   cohort is 2023/24 on a three-year degree. If the spring graduation sessions
   still belong to a.y. 2025/26 you keep the +2 points on the final mark and
   pay no further fees; if not, you lose both. Ask the segreteria — this is not
   published anywhere I can read.
